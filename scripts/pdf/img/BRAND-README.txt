Final Circular C / Helvetica Bold

SELECTED DESIGN
Circular C made of three concentric circular arcs. The long straight sections
have been removed. A 72-degree opening and radial square cuts make the letter
more explicit. Radii are 96, 74, and 52 units; stroke width is 14 units with
8-unit gaps. The visible silhouette is centred on the square artboard.
Wordmark: actual Helvetica Bold, weight 700, tracking -0.015em.
The letterforms and tracking are unchanged. The words are moved closer to the C
in the primary two-line and wide one-line lockups. Stacked and symbol-only
artwork are unchanged from the selected plain circular C.

USE
Preserve the proportions. Leave at least two symbol-stroke widths of clear space
around the artwork. Prefer the symbol alone at 32 px and above; check finer sizes
on the actual display. At small sizes, the wide logo is usually easier to read
than the stacked layout. PNGs are RGB screen assets; SVGs scale without loss.
For web use, an SVG in an image element preserves the exact lettering without
requiring a webfont. Add appropriate alt text or an accessible link label.
